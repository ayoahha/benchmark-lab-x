# Excerpts from the LedgerCloud service agreement (Section 12)

Agreement between Northbridge Systems Ltd and Atlantic Electrical Supply Ltd, in force on 14 April 2026.

## Clause 12.1 — Availability

Northbridge Systems guarantees monthly platform availability of at least 99.5%, measured 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Interruptions caused by scheduled maintenance are excluded from the calculation provided that the client was notified at least 72 hours in advance.

## Clause 12.2 — Service credits

If the availability rate specified in Clause 12.1 is not met, the client is entitled to a credit equal to 5% of the monthly subscription excluding VAT for each started 0.5 percentage point below the guaranteed threshold, capped at 30% of the monthly subscription excluding VAT.

## Clause 12.3 — Communication during a major incident

For any major incident, defined as a total platform outage, Northbridge Systems informs the client within one hour of detecting the incident and then provides a progress update every two hours until service is fully restored.
