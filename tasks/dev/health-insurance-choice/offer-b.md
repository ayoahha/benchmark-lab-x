**2026 rates and benefits brochure**

---

## About Clarity

Clarity Health Mutual provides supplemental health insurance for employees and their families. Family Balance Plus is designed for households focused on hospitalization and optical care while keeping out-of-pocket costs under control.

---

## Contributions

Monthly rates incl. tax, mainland France, based on adult age at enrollment.

| Profile | Age 35–39 | Age 40–44 | Age 45–49 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adult | €85 | **€92** | €101 |
| Spouse (same age band) | €85 | **€92** | €101 |
| Each child (age 0–17) | €28 | €28 | €28 |

**Example household: 2 adults age 40–44 + 2 children:**  
92 + 92 + 28 + 28 = **€240/month**.

Monthly payment by direct debit. The first month is due on the effective date.

## Notices and exclusions (read carefully)

**Standard exclusions:** services not reimbursed by statutory health insurance unless an allowance is expressly provided; stays in a non-contracted facility except in an emergency; cosmetic surgery.

**Sports and risks.** Hospitalization costs resulting from participation in an officially organized sports competition are excluded unless the “Pro Sport” option is purchased; that option is not included in Family Balance Plus.

**Renewal.** Rates may be revised on 1 January each year based on changes in the mutual insurer's medical claims; notice is provided 2 months before renewal.
