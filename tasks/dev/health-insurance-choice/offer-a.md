# ClearPath Health Mutual — “Family Serenity” plan

**Synthetic provider:** ClearPath Health Mutual (CPHM)  
**Range:** Family Serenity — level 2  
**Commercial document — March 2026 edition**  
**Valid for enrollment between 1 April and 31 August 2026**

---

## 1. Overview

Family Serenity is designed for households with children seeking a balance among optical care, hospitalization, and a controlled budget. Benefits apply under the general statutory scheme and supplement statutory health insurance reimbursements.

Enrollment is available for Adult 1, a spouse, and up to 4 dependent children under age 18.

## 7. General terms — excerpts

**Article 12 — Cancellation.** Annual cancellation on the renewal date with 2 months' notice, or under the statutory conditions for cancelling supplemental health insurance after the first year.

**Article 14 — Territorial scope.** Benefits apply in mainland France and the French overseas departments and regions. Care in the European Union is reimbursed on the basis of French rates upon submission of benefit statements.

**Article 18 — General exclusions.** Excluded: cosmetic surgery not covered by statutory health insurance; rejuvenation treatments; consequences of reckless activities (extreme sports listed in Appendix 3).

**Article 19 — Optical limit for minors.** For members under age 16, the progressive-lens allowance is reduced to €120 per pair for standard pediatric equipment. This limit does not apply to adults on the policy.
