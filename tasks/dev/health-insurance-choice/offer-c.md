# United Valleys Mutual — “Family Horizon 3” policy

**Synthetic provider:** United Valleys Mutual (UVM)  
**Group policy open to individual enrollment:** Family Horizon, level 3  
**Benefits and rates brochure — spring 2026**

---

## Your coverage at a glance

Family Horizon 3 is designed for households that prioritize hospitalization and private-room comfort while retaining strong optical allowances.

---

## Monthly rates incl. tax

Single mainland zone. Rate based on the primary adult's age at enrollment.

| | Adult | Spouse | Child |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Under age 40 | €88 | €88 | €35 |
| Age 40–49 | **€95** | **€95** | €35 |
| Age 50–59 | €112 | €112 | €35 |

**Typical household: 2 adults age 40–49 + 2 children:**  
95 + 95 + 35 + 35 = **€260/month**.

Contribution for the third and each subsequent child: €20/month; not applicable to the typical 2+2 household.

## Special provisions — brochure footer

**Administration contribution.** A contribution of €2/month/policy is included in the displayed rates.

**Indexation.** Contributions may change annually.

**Exclusions.** In addition to statutory and regulatory exclusions, Family Horizon 3 does not cover stays in long-term care beyond 90 continuous days, cosmetic procedures, or hospitalization related to a condition reported inaccurately at enrollment.

**Extended waiting-period clause — contact lenses.** Contact-lens allowances become available only after **twelve (12) months** of continuous membership. No contact-lens reimbursement is payable during the first eleven months, including when a prescription is renewed. This provision takes precedence over the general 4-month optical waiting period for the contact-lens benefit only.
