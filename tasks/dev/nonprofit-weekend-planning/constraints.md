## Numbered constraints

**C1.** The event must have exactly **two plenary sessions** in Room A: one on Saturday morning (09:00–12:00) and one on Sunday morning (09:30–12:30). No plenary session may take place in the afternoon.

**C2.** The event must have exactly **three workshops** in Room B, each lasting one full bookable slot. At least one workshop must take place on Saturday and at least one on Sunday.

**C3.** Amelia Foster (B1) runs reception throughout **every plenary session**. She therefore cannot be assigned elsewhere during a plenary slot. She is available on both days from 08:30 to 18:00 only and is unavailable after 18:00.

**C4.** Kevin Bennett (B2) is the only person qualified to manage sound during plenary sessions. He must attend **every** plenary session. He is unavailable on **Sunday afternoon** because of a family commitment.

**C5.** Sophie Marshall (B3) and Nadia Ellis (B5) are the only workshop facilitators. **Every** workshop must be led by one of them, but not both on the same workshop; one workshop has one facilitator. Sophie is available all day Saturday and Sunday morning only. Nadia is available all day Sunday and Saturday afternoon only, not Saturday morning.

**C6.** Julian Reed (B4) runs the catering point during **every workshop slot** in Room B and during the Saturday 17:30–19:30 slot if that slot is used. He is **not** available on Sunday morning.

**C7.** Thomas Grant (B6) handles setup: he must be present **30 minutes before** every plenary session and every workshop to open the relevant room. He is available Saturday 08:00–20:00 and Sunday 09:00–17:00.

**C8.** Room B cannot be used at the same time as a plenary session in Room A on **Sunday morning**: the community centre's house rules require complete quiet throughout the building during the Sunday-morning closing plenary. Room A and Room B may operate in parallel on Saturday morning.

**C9.** Room A must be reserved during the Saturday 17:30–19:30 slot for a **volunteer review meeting** lasting the entire slot. Amelia (B1), Kevin (B2), and Thomas (B6) must attend. The other volunteers may attend but are not required.

**C10.** Nadia Ellis (B5) must facilitate **exactly two** of the three workshops. Sophie Marshall (B3) facilitates the third.
